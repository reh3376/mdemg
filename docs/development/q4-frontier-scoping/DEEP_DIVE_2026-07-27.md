## 2. Quality-frontier findings (live evidence)

### Finding 1: Guidance follow rate is stuck at ~11%, **and the frontier is corpus quality, not plumbing**

7-day slice of `constraint_outcomes` on the live loop:

| Scope | n | followed % | ignored % | contradicted % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raw (all guidance types) | 1,885 | **10.66%** | 62.9% | 1.4% |
| Actionable-only (constraint + correction) | 680 | **11.03%** | 82.8% | 1.4% |

Per-type breakdown:

| Type | n | followed | ignored | contradicted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pattern | 846 | 11.35% | 54.9% | 1.7% |
| constraint | 577 | 10.92% | **79.7%** | 0.7% |
| learning | 245 | 9.39% | 62.0% | 2.0% |
| risk | 106 | 5.66% | 13.2% | 2.8% |
| **correction** | 103 | 11.65% | **86.4%** | 0.0% |
| concept | 8 | 12.50% | 87.5% | 0.0% |

**Two calibrations frame this:**
1. **JIMINY-ACTIONABILITY-COMPLIANCE-CREDIT-001 recalibrated** the honest steady
   state to **~13%** — the live 11.03% is roughly on-band (below by 2pp, likely
   within noise post-clause activation).
2. **The operator's stated goal** (per JIMINY-RELEVANCE-001) was **>90% follow
   on actionable guidance.** Gap = 8×.

Since three consecutive JIMINY-* sprints have converged on ~13% as the ceiling
under current architecture (JIMINY-ACTIONABILITY-001, JIMINY-CORPUS-001,
JIMINY-ACTIONABILITY-COMPLIANCE-CREDIT-001), **the ceiling is not classifier
mechanics — it is corpus quality and semantic-relevance-of-surfaced-guidance.**
Two sprint-lines investigated this and both concluded the same thing.

### Finding 2: 25% of live guidance outcomes are labeled by a classifier that produces **0% follow rate**

Classifier-source split in `constraint_outcomes` 7d:

| classifier_source | n | pct_followed |
|---|---|---|
| llm | 1,100 | **16.18%** |
| heuristic | **473** | **0.00%** |
| tier1 | 312 | 7.37% |

**Every single one of 473 `heuristic`-labeled outcomes over 7d was labeled as
NOT-followed.** Either the heuristic path is systematically wrong (measurement
artifact deflating headline follow rate), or the heuristic path is only invoked
where the LLM correctly declined and the answer really is "ignored" (semantically
correct but a signal-quality asymmetry).

Interesting comparison: the `guidance_training_rows` table (JIMINY-RELEVANCE-001
corpus, feeds the HITL/FT loop) has only **0.03% heuristic rows** (2 of 6,568) —
GUIDANCE-AUDIT-001 relabels those away. But `constraint_outcomes` (the alert-rule
+ dashboard input) still shows 25% heuristic 7d. **Two different write paths,
one is being upgraded, one isn't.** Worth a small investigation sprint.

### Finding 3: HITL curation has stalled — 2 grades in 7d against 5 unreviewed contradicted-drafts

HITL activity 7d: **2 review_grades total, both on `contradicted_drafts`.**
5 `contradicted_correction_drafts` sit in `status='pending'`, oldest from
Jul 21 (6 days). These are Jiminy-authored high-signal drafts (both violated
guidance AND the offending action captured; ready-to-review contrast pairs).
They are precisely the "surprise-weighted" signal HEBB-ETA cares about, and
they're just sitting there.

Two failure modes:
- **Operator-behavioral** — the review UI isn't in the workflow yet
- **Missing auto-curation** — the drafts are well-formed enough that an
  LLM-graded pass could clear the queue at low quality risk

Neither is a shipped-sprint follow-up. Both would need a new small sprint.

### Finding 4: Recursive-retrain is idle by design — but has never fired organically

`ft_training_cycles` ledger 7d: **9 cycles, ALL from 2026-07-23** (the
FTLOOP-DRILL-001 day). Breakdown: 3 `promoted`, 5 `rolled_back`, 1 `failed`.
Since the drill, zero cycles. Currently serving version: **`mdemg-llm-v1`
deployed 2026-05-03, score 0.8655** — the pre-drill baseline; every
drill-promoted version rolled back.

Benchmark freshness:
- Latest: `t9czjxhg` on 2026-07-23 (4.1d), aggregate **0.9156**
- Prior: `ed062ea8` on 2026-07-21 (6.3d), aggregate **0.9188**
- Delta: −0.003 (drift threshold is 0.05, so 17× under trigger)

**The tripwire has fired 5 times in one day** (drill), proving the
auto-rollback layer works. **The organic-drift trigger has never been
tested end-to-end** — no cycle has been created by the drift condition
in production. The current drift signal isn't landing (my query returned
NULL for `mdemg_ft_production_drift` in the last 15 min).

This is a validation gap, not a defect — the machinery is proven by drill.
But the "loop actually learns from real drift" claim is unverified.

### Finding 5: Corrections producer is working, but production is bootstrap-stalled

JIMINY-CORRECTION-PRODUCER-001 shipped 32 L1 correction nodes on 2026-07-20.
7 days later: **35 active corrections (+3), zero created in the last 7 days**.
The producer requires L0 observations with `obs_type='correction'`. The obs
producer for this is the operator's own explicit "CORRECTION:" utterances via
`/v1/conversation/correct` and the JIMINY-CONTRADICTED-BRIDGE-001 auto-flow.
Neither has produced new obs in 7d.

**Correction guidance has the highest actionable-ignore rate (86.4%).** The
35 rules that exist look useful (from CLAUDE.md pins: never-commit-to-main,
`max_completion_tokens` for gpt-5.x, sprint plans belong in `docs/development/`,
never `mdemg db start`) but the follow rate suggests either the surface
composition is wrong or the rules aren't matching the contexts they should.

### Finding 7: Guardrail-producer is landing real production rows

`guardrail.evaluate` 7d: **59 calls, avg 3.9s latency, 0% error.** Last call
20:17 UTC today. GUARDRAIL-PRODUCER-001 shipped 2026-07-22 as the 16th LLM

is now the bottleneck between the producer and the retrain trigger. HITL
review activity 7d = 2 total. That's not it.

## 4. Recommendation

**Ship candidates 1–3 as the Q4 committed core (~10 days total).** They target
the three most-load-bearing frontier gaps:
- Corpus quality (the identified follow-rate ceiling driver)
- Measurement integrity (25%-heuristic split poisons the headline metric)
- Recursive-loop organic validation (never proven)

Then re-evaluate. Candidates 4 and 6 are research-class — worth queueing but
run *after* seeing whether 1–3 move the needle. Candidates 5 and 9 are cheap
closeouts. Candidate 7 will emerge naturally if 1 succeeds.

**Should the workflow-generated Q4 roadmap (Option B) be launched?**

**My honest read: NO, not yet.** The Q3 workflow was justified because the
substrate was a maze of unknown silent-failure defects that needed
26-agent-fanout to inventory. The substrate now works. The Q4 frontier is
narrow enough (guidance quality + measurement integrity + validation) that
you can pick 3–5 sprints from this doc and start next week. A workflow would
spend ~3.8M tokens re-discovering what this deep dive found; the frontier is
a research question, not an inventory problem.

**If any of the top-3 findings surprises you and you'd rather have wider
independent coverage before committing sprint capacity, Option B (workflow)
is defensible then. Otherwise start with candidate 1.**
